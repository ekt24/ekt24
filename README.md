## Hi there 👋

I’m a recent BSc grad interested in data management, data analytics, and research project coordination—especially in clinical research! 🧬📊

I get excited about making messy data make sense, finding useful insights, and helping teams stay organized. Getting to do all that while supporting research that could improve patient care? Even better. 💛

My interests take me in lots of directions, so here are a few course projects I’ve built along the way! 👇
